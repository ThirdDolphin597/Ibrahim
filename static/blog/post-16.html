Starting the Next ROV

Last week in shop, I started thinking about designing the next remotely operated vehicle (ROV) for Sunk Robotics. I discussed it with my teammates and teacher. Tim already has an ROV design, so I'm not starting from scratch. I want to optimize his design further and make the next ROV the very best it can be. Honestly, I want it to be overkill.

Learning Hydrodynamics and CFD

One thing I want to improve is how the ROV moves through water. I'm learning hydrodynamics through MIT OpenCourseWare and figuring out computational fluid dynamics (CFD) alongside my Fusion design work. I want to use the math and simulations to compare shapes, see how water flows around them, and understand how much drag they create instead of just choosing something that looks streamlined.

Learning the CFD tools in the shop.

I also want to test the designs in a water tunnel and compare the results with the CFD predictions. I'm still learning the tools and theory, so I haven't found the best shape or done that testing yet. I'm considering making this a science fair project, bringing together the mathematical analysis, simulations, and physical testing.

Finishing the Camera Mount

I also helped finish a piece Tim made to mount the camera inside the front of the tube. I deburred it and decided to sandblast it. Tim suggested oiling it with machining oil, so I did that and then cleaned it.

I absolutely loved the finish. I've decided I want to use the same sandblasted and oiled finish on the ROV we make for MATE.
